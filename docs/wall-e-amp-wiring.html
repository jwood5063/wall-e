Wall-E voice bench — MAX98357A amp added to the ReSpeaker HAT speaker

The speaker moves off the ReSpeaker HAT's JST socket and onto a MAX98357A I2S amp. The amp taps the same I2S playback bus the HAT codec already uses — no new data path, no software change. Power is 5 V from Pi header pin 2.

SD: leave as the breakout ships — the onboard 1 MΩ pull-up to Vin sets the (L+R)/2 mono mix. SD below 0.16 V = shutdown; don't ground it.

Start at 9 dB gain and moderate volume: full-scale 3.2 W into a 3 W speaker leaves no headroom.

Warning — Class-D bridge output

Neither speaker lead is ground. Never tie either lead to GND — the speaker connects only between the amp's + and − screw terminals, and the HAT's JST speaker socket stays empty.

Notes

NO SOFTWARE CHANGE

The amp listens to the same I2S playback stream as the HAT codec; ALSA still sees card seeed2micvoicec. Both outputs play at once — that's why the speaker moves to the amp and the HAT's JST socket is left empty.

WHY ADD THE AMP

On the V2.0 HAT the JST speaker output comes off the codec's headphone path (weak). The MAX98357A delivers up to ~3 W into the 4 Ω speaker at 5 V — real volume for the Wall-E voice.

GAIN & HEADROOM

Start at 9 dB gain and moderate volume: full-scale 3.2 W into a 3 W speaker leaves no headroom. Raise gain later only if the bench voice is still too quiet at sane ALSA levels.
